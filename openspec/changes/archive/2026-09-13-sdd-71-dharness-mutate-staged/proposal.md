# Proposal: SDD-71 Frontend Staged Mutation via `dharness mutate --staged`

## Intent

`frontend/scripts/dlinter-mutation-staged.mjs` (151 lines, job at `lefthook.yml:291-294`) is a measured silent no-op in every linked-worktree commit: the hook's `GIT_DIR` makes `--show-toplevel` resolve to `frontend/`, so every production path is rejected (9578318: 6 files, 0 mutants — Engram 9470). This defect class has been fixed four times. The owner chose upstream `dharness mutate --staged` over a manual job or whole-file `{staged_files}` (Engram 9446).

Success: a real `git commit` from a linked worktree blocks any in-scope Survived or NoCoverage mutant and passes everything else.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Replace the job with `dharness mutate --staged --exclude-prefix src/test/ --concurrency 4` (`root: frontend`; the exclude list grew at apply time to keep the scope to `src/`, see design AD2), kept in the repo-owned `lefthook.yml` (`.dharness/lefthook.yml` is regenerated by `dharness sync`); drop the `dlinter:owned` marker.
- Delete the script; through `npm pkg`, remove `test:mutation:staged` and repoint `test:mutation`.
- Rename `stryker.dlinter.json` → `stryker.config.json` (the only names dharness detects); remove `thresholds`. Keep `vitest.dlinter-mutation.mts` (NotificationTable.windowing exclusion).
- Verdict D1: any in-scope Survived/NoCoverage fails, no score (8c3f30a scored 85.7 with a killable survivor; the two statuses flip with unrelated lines — Engram 9460, 9465, 9467). Escape hatch: `// Stryker disable next-line <mutator>: <reason>`, printed as Ignored.
- Pin the new job in `tools/checkgofilesize/repository_policy_test.go` (RED first).
- Rewrite docs made false: CLAUDE.md note 16; AGENTS.md:48 (already stale) and :75; `docs/mutation-testing.md` frontend section and :82 (keep ditto's `--threshold 0.80`, drop the `break: 80` rationale); `docs/fallow-usage.md:21`; `frontend/.fallowrc.json` comments; `merge_gate_test.go:52` comment; dated note in `postmortem-silent-no-ops.md`; one `log-lesson.mjs` line.

### Out of Scope
- ditto (Engram 9462), Stryker filings, dharness internals.
- Types-only commits (1.4%): upstream M11 decision (Engram 9472).
- `vitest.related:false`: full-suite dry run hit 306 s > 5-min timeout (M10a).
